Strategic Protocols: Comprehensive Game Guide

Welcome to CyberTic Evolution, a next-generation approach to the classic game of Tic Tac Toe. While the foundational rules remain the same, our system introduces a progressive learning curve designed to test human logic against machine efficiency.

The Core Objective

The goal is simple: align three of your symbols (X) in a contiguous line—horizontally, vertically, or diagonally—before the Artificial Intelligence (O) completes its own sequence.

The 100 Tiers of Artificial Intelligence

  • Levels 1-30 (Easy): The AI is prone to errors, often missing obvious blocks. Designed for beginners.
  • Levels 31-60 (Normal): The AI begins recognizing immediate threats. It will actively attempt to block your horizontal and vertical alignments.
  • Levels 61-89 (Difficult): At this stage, the AI prioritizes high-value board positions, such as the center tile and corners, significantly reducing human victory chances.
  • Levels 90-100 (Impossible): The system activates the Minimax Algorithm. This recursive decision-making protocol from game theory simulates all possible future moves to ensure the AI never loses. A draw is your best outcome.

Multiplayer and Local Storage

Your current level progress is saved in your browser's local storage (localStorage). For online multiplayer, we use PeerJS to establish a direct WebRTC connection between you and your opponent. Game moves are transmitted directly peer-to-peer; we do not store chat logs or match history on any central servers.
